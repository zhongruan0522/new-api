---
title: MiniMax Voice Management
---

The `/minimax/voice-management` route is used to manage MiniMax custom voice resources.

Related features require administrators to enable and configure **System Settings → Models & Routing → MiniMax**:

- MiniMax feature toggle.
- Model redirection.
- Voice whitelist.
- Custom voice toggle, group, and billing model.
- Emotion tags and tone-word mapping.

If the sidebar entry is missing, check whether the MiniMax module is enabled in **System Settings → Site & Branding → Sidebar Modules**.
